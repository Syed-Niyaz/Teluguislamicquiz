Place your badge icon files in this folder.

Naming: use the badge id or its iconKey (case-insensitive; spaces/dashes ignored).
Examples:
  niyyat.svg
  talib.svg
  sabr.svg
  rah.svg
  nurani.svg
  fath.svg
  jawahir.svg
  istiqamat.svg
  barakat.svg
  nurul.svg
Or: sprout.png, book.svg, moon.svg, star.svg, sparkles.svg, medal.svg,
    gem.svg, landmark.svg, crown.svg, certificate.svg

These credits are also shown on each badge card and in its detail popup.

A "badge-" or "icon-" prefix is allowed too (e.g. badge-niyyat.svg).
Supported: .svg .png .webp .jpg .jpeg

Files are detected automatically at build time; no component changes needed.
Unlocked icons are shown inside an emerald medallion; locked icons are
muted blue-grey, blurred and dimmed.
